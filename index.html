Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

In short

Devkumar Mukhiya Calculator does not collect, sell or share your personal information. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

Data stored on your device

Your calculation history, the interest, service charge and insurance settings, and your theme choice are saved only on your device. Use "Clear history" or uninstall the app to remove them.

No accounts, ads or tracking

The app has no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no trackers. The calculator and the loan schedule work fully offline, and your calculations are never sent anywhere.

Feedback

"Send feedback" opens your own email app with the message you wrote. It is sent only if you press send there.

Files and printing

"Save PDF" stores the schedule in your phone's Downloads folder, and "Print" uses Android's print service. This happens only when you tap those buttons.

Permissions

The app does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, location or photos.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
